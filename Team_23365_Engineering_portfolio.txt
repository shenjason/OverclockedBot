Robot#3 Page 4 Most Innovative Design
Outtake System: Our outtake is a two-axis
system, which we can rotate our arm and claw
which different with other teams who can only
rotate the arm. We used two Axone mini servos
for our outtake arm and three gobuilda torque
servos for our claw and upper arm. When our
robot want to grab the specimen from the
observation zone, our outtake arm first swivel to
the back of our machine and grip the specimen.
After this, both of our arm and claw will rotate
180 degrees (change to original position)and push
to specimen on the higher chamber.
Intake Claw: Our outtake claw design
is based on the principle of gears
interlocking and rotating. There is a
screw hole between two parts of our
claw so we can easily attach them.
They are small but really sharp and
can open really wide so we can grab
the sample really tied and accurate.
Outtake Claw: Our outtake claw is similar
to out outtake claw because they have
same design concept. We just made the
Intake System : Out intake system is a
clamping part shorter and more rounded
three axis system which we can grab
so it is not easily been broken.
the sample at every side and height.
When we want to grab the samples in
the middle, we extend the intake slides
and the intake system will rotate out.
(first rotation) Our claw will rotate any
degree or side to best fit the sample
placement. (second rotation) After
these, the middle part of our intake will
rotate down in order to make our claw
grab the sample. (third rotation) We
designed a linker which connect with
our intake slides. We use two Axone
mini servos for our back part, two
